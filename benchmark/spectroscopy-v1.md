# DESI DR1 spectroscopy coverage

The audit searched 26 validation fields and 1,425 candidates in their baseline cones. It used the public DESI DR1 Main Survey bright and dark Redrock files.
The archive returned 19 files, 43 HTTP 404 responses for absent files, and 0 failed requests.

- 17 candidates in 7 fields had an unambiguous, usable galaxy redshift.
- 13 candidates in 7 fields had a usable redshift and no DESI secondary-target flag.
- 10 fields had a ZTF transient-template redshift. 0 of them also had at least one usable candidate redshift without a secondary-target flag, and 0 had two or more.

A missing secondary flag is a provenance screen. Field-level independence still requires checking targeting and the SN measurement. These counts describe coverage and leave host identities unchanged.
